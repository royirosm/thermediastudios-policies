Privacy Policy

Effective July 24, 2026 Contact thermediastudios@gmail.com

TheRMediaStudios operates an internal video publishing tool used to prepare and post short-form video content to social platforms, including TikTok, on behalf of the studio's own content brands. This policy explains what the tool accesses, why, and how it's handled.

1

Who this applies to

This tool is operated solely by TheRMediaStudios for its own use. It is not distributed to the public and does not have external end users — every TikTok account it connects to belongs to TheRMediaStudios or one of its own content brands. It is not used to access, collect, or process data belonging to any third party.

2

What we access

When a studio-owned TikTok account is connected, the tool requests:

  • Basic account info (user.info.basic) — to confirm which account is connected and display its name/handle in our internal dashboard.
  • Video posting (video.publish) — to upload and publish pre-produced video files to that account via TikTok's Content Posting API.

The tool does not read your TikTok feed, followers, messages, analytics beyond what TikTok returns for posted content, or any data belonging to accounts other than the ones explicitly connected by the studio.

3

How data is stored

OAuth access and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest and stored on infrastructure controlled by TheRMediaStudios. Video files and captions are studio-produced content staged temporarily for upload and are not shared with any third party other than TikTok itself, as part of the publish action the studio initiates.

We do not sell data, share it with advertisers, or use it for purposes beyond operating the studio's own publishing workflow.
4

Retention & revocation

Tokens are retained until the studio disconnects an account, which can be done at any time from the tool's internal settings, or until TikTok's own token expiry applies. Disconnecting immediately stops the tool from accessing that account, and revokes the tool's stored credentials for it.

5

Changes to this policy

If the tool's scope of access changes materially, this page will be updated and the effective date above revised accordingly.
