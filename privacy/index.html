Privacy Policy

Effective July 24, 2026 Updated October 9, 2026 Contact thermediastudios@gmail.com

TheRMediaStudios operates an internal video publishing tool ("Video Pipeline") used to prepare and post short-form video content to social platforms, including TikTok and YouTube, on behalf of the studio's own content brands. This policy explains what the tool accesses, why, and how it's handled.

1

Who this applies to

This tool is operated solely by TheRMediaStudios for its own use. It is not distributed to the public and does not have external end users — every TikTok account and YouTube channel it connects to belongs to TheRMediaStudios or one of its own content brands. It is not used to access, collect, or process data belonging to any third party.

2

What we access on TikTok

When a studio-owned TikTok account is connected, the tool requests:

  • Basic account info (user.info.basic) — to confirm which account is connected and display its name/handle in our internal dashboard.
  • Video posting (video.publish) — to upload and publish pre-produced video files to that account via TikTok's Content Posting API.

The tool does not read your TikTok feed, followers, messages, analytics beyond what TikTok returns for posted content, or any data belonging to accounts other than the ones explicitly connected by the studio.

3

Google and YouTube user data

The tool connects to the studio's own YouTube channels using Google OAuth. After the studio owner grants consent, it may use these Google permissions, and only for channels the studio owns:

  • Account email and basic profile — to identify which Google account is connected.
  • Manage your YouTube account (youtube, youtube.force-ssl, youtube.upload) — to upload the studio's own videos, set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and schedule, post comments on them, and set end screens.
  • YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly, yt-analytics-monetary.readonly) — to show the studio how its own videos perform, including revenue reports where available.

The tool does not read other people's accounts, subscriber lists, private messages or watch history.

Use. Google user data is used only to publish the studio's own videos and to show the studio how its own videos perform. It is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to build user profiles, to train generalized AI or machine learning models, or for any purpose unrelated to these features.

Storage. OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest on infrastructure controlled by TheRMediaStudios. Analytics reports are kept on the same infrastructure.

Sharing. The tool does not share Google user data with third parties. Data is sent only to Google and YouTube as part of actions the studio performs, and is read by people at the studio only to operate the studio's own channels.

TheRMediaStudios' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. By using YouTube API Services through this tool, the studio is also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
4

How data is stored

OAuth access and refresh tokens (TikTok and Google) are encrypted at rest and stored on infrastructure controlled by TheRMediaStudios. Video files and captions are studio-produced content staged temporarily for upload and are not shared with any third party other than the platform being posted to (TikTok or YouTube), as part of the publish action the studio initiates.

We do not sell data, share it with advertisers, or use it for purposes beyond operating the studio's own publishing workflow.
5

Retention & revocation

Tokens are retained until the studio disconnects an account, which can be done at any time from the tool's internal settings, or until the platform's own token expiry applies. Disconnecting immediately stops the tool from accessing that account and deletes the tool's stored credentials for it.

Google access can also be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To request deletion of any stored data, contact thermediastudios@gmail.com.

6

Changes to this policy

If the tool's scope of access changes materially, this page will be updated and the effective date above revised accordingly.
